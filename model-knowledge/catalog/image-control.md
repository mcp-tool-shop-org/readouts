### Qwen-Image-ControlNet-Union (InstantX) · `recommended` · frontier

A full (not LoRA) unified ControlNet for the Apache-2.0 Qwen-Image 20B base, supporting canny, soft-edge, depth, and pose in one model. Because BOTH the base and the ControlNet are Apache-2.0, this is the clean commercial path for game-asset structure control (concept-to-pose, depth-driven 3D-asset texturing) that Flux's non-commercial ControlNets cannot legally serve. Trained from scratch (50K steps, 10M images at 1328px). Pairs with ComfyUI native Qwen-Image workflows; on 32 GB you run base+ControlNet in bf16 comfortably, or GGUF/Nunchaku quant for headroom.
