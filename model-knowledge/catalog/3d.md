## Detail

### Hi3DGen (Stable3DGen) · `recommended` · frontier
The geometry-quality leader for single-image-to-3D as of mid-2025/2026. Its normal-bridging approach recovers sharp edges and fine surface detail that feed-forward models smooth away, making it the best local choice when silhouette/geometry accuracy matters more than raw speed. Outputs clean geometry (texture is a separate downstream step). MIT-licensed with NVIDIA deps deliberately removed for commercial cleanliness, and it ships a ComfyUI node — so it drops straight into a 5090 game-asset pipeline as the high-fidelity geometry front end. Best paired with Hunyuan3D-2.1 PBR bake + UniRig rigging downstream.
- **Dev:** Stable-X (academic team; builds on Microsoft TRELLIS) · **Arch:** Normal-bridged latent diffusion — image->normal (NiRNE) then normal->geometry (NoRLD), on a TRELLIS-derived structured-latent backbone with NVIDIA-lib deps (kaolin/nvdiffrast/flexicube) removed to enable clean commercial use · **Params:** TRELLIS-class backend + NiRNE normal estimator (sub-2B geometry core) · **Released:** 2025-03 (arXiv 2503.22236); ComfyUI node + Stable3DGen repo mid-2025 · **Disk:** 6.0 GB · **VRAM:** min 8.0 GB / rec 12.0 GB
- **License:** MIT — commercial: **yes**. MIT across model + code. Notably the maintainers explicitly stripped TRELLIS's NVIDIA-licensed dependencies (kaolin, nvdiffrast, flexicube) specifically to make commercial use clean — a deliberate commercial-safety move. No revenue cap, no region lock.
- **Best for:** Hero props / character base meshes where silhouette and edge fidelity are critical (game-asset, fit 5) ; Geometry front end feeding a retopo->UV->PBR->rig pipeline (game-asset, fit 5) ; High-detail 3D for marketing renders / product viz (marketing, fit 3)
- **Speed/notes:** Two-stage (normal estimation -> geometry); slower than feed-forward TripoSR but the normal-bridging step is exactly what buys the geometric fidelity. Minutes-scale per asset on a 5090, not seconds.
- **Verify:** verdict=confirmed | currency=current | Stable-X/Hi3DGen live. LICENSE verbatim: adapted from Trellis (MIT) and NVIDIA libs kaolin/nvdiffrast/flexicube removed for commercial use - MIT, commercial-yes, TRELLIS backbone all confirmed. Project page names NiRNE (Noise-injected Regressive Normal Estimation) and NoRLD (Normal-Regularized Latent Diffusion) verbatim. ICCV 2025 (arXiv 2503.22236), benchmarked above Hunyuan3D-2.0/Trellis/TripoSG/Clay/Tripo-2.5/Dora on fidelity - geometry-leader claim and currency hold. First-party ComfyUI node exists (Stable-X/ComfyUI-Hi3DGen). Only soft spec: 8GB VRAM not stated on any official source; TRELLIS-derived so plausible (32GB covers it).
- **Sources:** [Hi3DGen: High-fidelity 3D Geometry Generation from Images via Normal Bridging](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.22236) — Hi3DGen generates high-fidelity 3D geometry from images via a normal-bridging framework: image-to-normal estimator (NiRNE), normal-to-geometry learning (NoRLD), plus a 3D data synthesis pipeline. ; [Stable-X/Hi3DGen (Stable3DGen) GitHub](https://github.com/Stable-X/Hi3DGen) — Stable3DGen/Hi3DGen is adapted from TRELLIS under MIT License, with NVIDIA dependencies (kaolin, nvdiffrast, flexicube) removed to enable commercial use. ; [Stable-X/trellis-normal-v0-1 model card](https://huggingface.co/Stable-X/trellis-normal-v0-1) — The Stable-X normal-conditioned TRELLIS model underpinning Hi3DGen is licensed MIT and is consumed by the official Stable-X/Hi3DGen Space and Stable3DGen variants. ; [Stable-X/ComfyUI-Hi3DGen](https://github.com/Stable-X/ComfyUI-Hi3DGen) — Hi3DGen ships a ComfyUI extension (IF_Trellis / IF_TrellisCheckpointLoader nodes) and is MIT-licensed.

### Hunyuan3D-2.1 · `recommended` · frontier
The first FULLY open-sourced Hunyuan3D with production-grade PBR texture synthesis (physically-grounded materials: metallic reflections, subsurface scattering). The go-to open model when texture realism matters more than topology. Image-to-3D, exports GLB/OBJ meshes plus separate PBR maps. Total download ~14.9 GB (DiT 7.37 GB fp16 + PaintPBR + VAE). On the 5090 the full shape+texture pipe peaks ~29 GB — fits 32 GB with room to spare; shape-only is ~10 GB VRAM. Deepest ComfyUI/LoRA ecosystem of any open 3D model (community node ComfyUI-Hunyuan3d-2-1, plus a GPU-poor fork). LICENSE CAVEAT below is the reason it is not an unconditional #1.

### TRELLIS.2-4B · `recommended` · frontier
Microsoft's late-2025 successor to TRELLIS and the current open-weights SOTA for single-image-to-3D. A 4B flow-matching transformer over a 'field-free' sparse-voxel (O-Voxel) latent that handles arbitrary topology (open surfaces, thin walls, non-manifold geometry) and outputs textured GLB with full PBR (base color, metallic, roughness, opacity). MIT-licensed = clean for commercial game assets. Runs on the 5090: needs >=24 GB; FP8 ComfyUI variants + tiled decoder give headroom on 32 GB. ComfyUI via visualbruno/ComfyUI-Trellis2 and PozzettiAndrea/ComfyUI-TRELLIS2 (Windows-11 tested) but requires gated Meta DINOv3 access. Caveat: raw meshes are dense tris, not watertight (hole-filling scripts provided) and not aesthetically RLHF-tuned, so budget a retopo/UV pass for hero assets.
- **Dev:** Microsoft · **Arch:** Flow-matching DiT + Sparse 3D VAE ('O-Voxel' field-free sparse voxels) · **Params:** 4B · **Released:** 2025-12-16 · **Disk:** 16.0 GB · **VRAM:** min 24.0 GB / rec 24.0 GB
- **License:** MIT (model + code). Note: optional nvdiffrast/nvdiffrec deps are non-commercial NVIDIA license. — commercial: **yes**. MIT on the model and inference code — commercial use, redistribution, and fine-tuning all permitted with no territory or MAU limit. Only watch-out: if you use the bundled nvdiffrast/nvdiffrec rendering utilities in a commercial pipeline, those carry NVIDIA's 1-way non-commercial source license; substitute an open renderer for baking.
- **Cloud:** local — MIT-clean (best-licensed mesh source) but needs a non-allowlisted wrapper + Linux/24GB build -> local 5090 only.
- **Best for:** Rapid concepting / batch iterating on props before committing to retopo (both, fit 5) ; Game props with complex/hollow/thin-wall topology (game-asset, fit 5) ; Environment kit-bashing blanks (high-poly source) (game-asset, fit 4) ; Marketing hero renders from a single concept image (marketing, fit 4)
- **Speed/notes:** On H100: ~3s at 512^3, ~17s at 1024^3, ~60s at 1536^3. On a 5090 expect well under a minute for typical 1024^3 props; the fastest high-quality open option for batch/iterate.

### TripoSG · `recommended` · frontier
VAST's flagship open image-to-3D geometry foundation model and the best all-round MIT-licensed geometry generator: 1.5B rectified-flow transformer over an SDF VAE, producing clean watertight meshes with strong generalization across object categories. The pragmatic default for game-asset geometry where you want high quality, MIT licensing, and good speed without Hi3DGen's two-stage cost. Integrated in ComfyUI-3D-Pack, and shares the VAST stack with UniRig (rigging) for a fully-MIT generate->rig path. Geometry only; texture/PBR is a downstream step.
- **Dev:** Tripo / VAST AI Research · **Arch:** Large-scale rectified-flow transformer with an SDF-based VAE (2048 latent tokens), cross-attention image conditioning, hybrid geometric supervision · **Params:** 1.5B · **Released:** 2025-02-10 (arXiv 2502.06608) · **Disk:** 6.0 GB · **VRAM:** min 8.0 GB / rec 12.0 GB

- **Speed/notes:** Rectified-flow transformer; fast high-quality geometry, well under a minute per asset on a 5090. Faster than Hi3DGen, marginally lower edge fidelity.

### PartCrafter · `recommended` · strong
The part-level breakthrough: from one RGB image it jointly generates multiple semantically distinct, geometrically separable meshes — not one fused blob. This is uniquely valuable for game work: separable armor pieces, swappable weapon attachments, articulated props, and kit-bash components come out pre-segmented, so they can be individually retopologized, textured, and rigged. ComfyUI-3D-Pack integrates it as a two-model pipeline (single-object mode with part segmentation, plus a scene mode), outputting a merged mesh and a ZIP of individual parts. MIT-licensed. The decomposition is the feature — for monolithic single objects, a plain geometry model is simpler.
- **Dev:** Peking University + ByteDance + Carnegie Mellon · **Arch:** Compositional latent diffusion — disentangled per-part latent token sets + hierarchical attention (intra-part and cross-part), jointly synthesizing multiple semantically distinct meshes in one pass · **Params:** Compositional latent diffusion transformer (TripoSG-lineage VAE) · **Released:** 2025-07-13 open-sourced (arXiv 2506.05573, NeurIPS 2025); scene variant 2025-07-23 · **Disk:** 6.0 GB · **VRAM:** min 8.0 GB / rec 16.0 GB
- **License:** MIT — commercial: **yes**. MIT (stated in paper and repo). Commercial-safe, finetune-friendly. A separate hosted partcrafter.net service has its own ToS, but the open weights/code are MIT.
- **Best for:** Separable game assets — modular armor, swappable weapon parts, articulated/multi-component props (game-asset, fit 5) ; Kit-bash component sets generated pre-segmented (game-asset, fit 5) ; Scene-level layouts decomposed into individual meshes (3D-Front-trained variant) (game-asset, fit 4)
- **Speed/notes:** Heavier than single-object models (it generates N parts jointly); VRAM scales with part count / token count, both tunable down. 32 GB gives generous room to push part count up — a real advantage of the 5090 here.
- **Verify:** verdict=confirmed | currency=current | wgsxm/PartCrafter live, MIT, NeurIPS 2025. Compositional Latent Diffusion Transformers generating multiple parts/objects from one RGB image in one shot - part-level/separable claim exact. VRAM at least 8GB confirmed (1024 tokens/part object, 2048 scene). ComfyUI-3D-Pack integration confirmed (listed in the Pack though not in PartCrafter README). Game-asset separable-parts use grounded.
- **Sources:** [wgsxm/PartCrafter GitHub](https://github.com/wgsxm/PartCrafter) — PartCrafter (NeurIPS 2025) is MIT-licensed, jointly generates multiple geometrically distinct part meshes from a single image, and runs on a CUDA GPU with >=8GB VRAM (reducible by lowering parts/tokens). ; [PartCrafter: Structured 3D Mesh Generation via Compositional Latent Diffusion Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.05573) — PartCrafter is the first structured 3D generative model jointly synthesizing multiple semantically meaningful, geometrically distinct meshes from one RGB image, via compositional latent diffusion transformers. ; [ComfyUI-3D-Pack PartCrafter integration](https://github.com/MrForExample/ComfyUI-3D-Pack) — ComfyUI-3D-Pack integrates PartCrafter as a two-model pipeline (single-object + scene modes), outputting merged meshes and per-part ZIP archives.
