### CapRL-InternVL3.5-8B · `recommended` · frontier

An 8B model whose image-captioning quality OUTPERFORMS Qwen2.5-VL-72B (a 9x larger model) - comparable perception accuracy and visual coverage, well-structured output, and notably fewer hallucinations, thanks to a caption-focused RL recipe (CapRL, ICLR 2026). If your single biggest job is captioning a corpus of reference images to train style LoRAs, run this: SOTA caption quality at 8B speed, Apache 2.0. Pair it with Qwen3-VL when you also need OCR/recognition/chat.
