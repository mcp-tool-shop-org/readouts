## Detail

### AMM (XLS-30 Automated Market Maker) · `amendment` · ● live ✓
Native, non-custodial Automated Market Maker integrated directly into the XRPL DEX. It uses a geometric-mean curve with weight 0.5, making it function as a constant-product market maker. Each AMM holds a pool of two assets, issues LPTokens to liquidity providers, and is automatically consulted alongside the order book so offers and payments route through whichever (pool, CLOB, or both) gives the best rate.
- **Chain:** xrpl-mainnet · **XLS:** XLS-30 · **Amendment:** AMM · **Enabled:** 2024-03-22 · **Maturity:** stable · **Use:** recommended
- **Key fields / API:** AMM ledger entry holds two pooled Amounts, TradingFee, LPTokenBalance, AuctionSlot, VoteSlots; transactions AMMCreate/Deposit/Withdraw/Vote/Bid/Delete/Clawback; LPToken uses a 160-bit hex currency code derived from a SHA-512 hash of the asset pair
- **Gotchas:** One AMM per asset pair (duplicates rejected). Both pooled IOUs require Default Ripple enabled on the issuer; LPTokens of another AMM cannot be pooled. Liquidity providers bear impermanent loss. AMM is OPEN-DEX only - it cannot fill permissioned offers/payments. Always seed at the true market ratio; off-ratio pools are immediately arbitraged.

- **Sources:** [Automated Market Makers - XRPL.org](https://xrpl.org/docs/concepts/tokens/decentralized-exchange/automated-market-makers) — XRPL's AMM is a geometric-mean (weight 0.5, constant-product) market maker integrated with the DEX so offers/payments auto-optimize across pool and order books; LPTokens use a 160-bit hash-derived currency code. ; [XLS-30 Live on Mainnet: AMM Integration on XRP Ledger is Here](https://dev.to/ripplexdev/xls-30-live-on-mainnet-amm-integration-on-xrp-ledger-is-here-4ac4) — The AMM amendment (XLS-30) became enabled on XRPL Mainnet on 2024-03-22. ; [XLS-0030 Automated Market Maker - XRPLF/XRPL-Standards](https://github.com/XRPLF/XRPL-Standards/tree/master/XLS-0030-automated-market-maker) — XLS-30 (status Final, authors Aanchal Malhotra and David Schwartz) specifies a non-custodial native AMM with a continuous auction mechanism for arbitrageurs.

### AMM <> CLOB Integration (auto-routing) · `pattern` · ● live ✓
Unlike standalone AMM DEXes, XLS-30 embeds the AMM in the native order book so a single OfferCreate or Payment is automatically optimized across pool liquidity, resting CLOB offers, or a blend of both for the best execution rate. Builders get aggregated liquidity for free - no separate router contract needed - and the AMM behaves like a synthetic offer the engine can consume alongside real offers.
- **Chain:** xrpl-mainnet · **XLS:** XLS-30 · **Amendment:** AMM · **Enabled:** 2024-03-22 · **Maturity:** stable · **Use:** recommended
- **Key fields / API:** No new fields - emergent from OfferCreate/Payment routing; the AMM is consulted as a synthetic counterparty during offer crossing and pathfinding
- **Gotchas:** fixAMMv1_1 was needed because low-quality CLOB offers could block AMM consumption; run current rippled. The blended routing only applies on the OPEN DEX - permissioned offers/payments are excluded from AMM liquidity. Quoting tools must simulate both sources to predict fills accurately.

### AMM-vs-CLOB choice for a game economy (architecture pattern) · `pattern` · ● live ✓
The architectural decision a studio actually has to make: AMM, CLOB, or both. Because XRPL routes a single offer/payment through pool + book + both for the best rate, the answer for a game economy is almost always BOTH, with a deliberate division of labor. Seed ONE AMM pool (GOLD/XRP or GOLD/RLUSD) as the always-on liquidity floor and fee-revenue engine — it needs zero maintenance, guarantees a tradeable price from day one, and serves the long tail of casual swaps. Let the CLOB form on top for depth: limit orders, no slippage at the quote, and the venue where guilds/power-users/MM bots trade size. Use the oracle as the price truth, the AMM as the floor, the CLOB as the depth, auto-bridging as the connectivity, and Permissioned DEX if access must be gated.
- **Chain:** xrpl-mainnet · **XLS:** XLS-30 · **Amendment:** AMM · **Enabled:** 2024-03-22 · **Maturity:** stable · **Use:** recommended
- **Key fields / API:** {"AMM_strengths": "always-on, zero-maintenance, fee revenue, no order management, good for casual long-tail", "AMM_weaknesses": "slippage in shallow pools, impermanent loss for the LP, manipulable spot for thin tokens", "CLOB_strengths": "no slippage at quote, limit orders, real depth, where size trades, no IL", "CLOB_weaknesses": "needs active makers (no built-in fee incentive), thin at launch, per-order reserve", "recommended": "ship AMM floor + let CLOB form on top; they share liquidity automatically"}
- **Gotchas:** Do NOT pick one and ignore the other thinking you must — the ledger already unifies them, so 'AMM-only' just means a worse experience for size traders and 'CLOB-only' means no day-one liquidity. Don't over-fund the AMM for a volatile launch token (impermanent loss can burn the treasury); start with a modest pool as the floor and let the CLOB carry growth. Never make in-game economy logic depend on AMM spot — that is the oracle's job (see price-oracle-xls47).

- **Verify:** cross-family(deepseek-v3.1:671b)=confirmed | net-check: live | currency=current | Integrated AMM+CLOB routing is live on mainnet via XLS-30d amendment.
- **Sources:** [Automated Market Makers — XRPL.org](https://xrpl.org/docs/concepts/tokens/decentralized-exchange/automated-market-makers) — The AMM is integrated with the order book so payments and offers automatically route through the pool, the order books, or both for the best rate; the AMM is a native ledger feature, not a smart contract. ; [Deep Dive into AMM Integration — XRPL.org](https://xrpl.org/blog/2024/deep-dive-into-amm-integration) — XLS-30 adds AMM functionality integrated with the existing decentralized exchange so offers and payments are optimized across the AMM and CLOB.

### AMMCreate · `transaction` · ● live ✓
Creates a new AMM instance for a given asset pair, transferring both seed assets from the sender into a newly created special AMM pseudo-account and issuing initial LPTokens to the creator. It also sets the pool's starting trading fee (0-1000, i.e. 0%-1%).
- **Chain:** xrpl-mainnet · **XLS:** XLS-30 · **Amendment:** AMM · **Enabled:** 2024-03-22 · **Maturity:** stable · **Use:** recommended
- **Key fields / API:** Amount (first asset), Amount2 (second asset), TradingFee (0-1000 = 0%-1%)
- **Gotchas:** Costs a special high fee equal to the incremental owner reserve (currently 0.2 XRP) to deter spam - same magnitude as AccountDelete, not the normal 10-drop fee. Fails if an AMM for the pair already exists. Both IOUs need Default Ripple; neither may be another AMM's LPToken. Seed at the correct market ratio or you donate to arbitrageurs immediately.

### AMMCreate (stand up the canonical game-token pool) · `transaction` · ● live ✓
The one-time transaction that mints the studio's GOLD/XRP (or GOLD/RLUSD) automated market maker. It creates the AMM AccountRoot + AMM ledger object, funds it with the two assets you supply (which seeds the starting price = ratio of the two amounts), sets the initial trading fee, and issues the first LPTokens to the creator. There can be exactly one AMM per asset pair on the whole ledger, so the studio's first AMMCreate for GOLD/XRP is the canonical, permanent venue — pick the seed amounts and the asset pairing deliberately because the starting price and fee are yours to set here.
- **Chain:** xrpl-mainnet · **XLS:** XLS-30 · **Amendment:** AMM · **Enabled:** 2024-03-22 · **Maturity:** stable · **Use:** recommended
- **Key fields / API:** {"Amount / Amount2": "the two assets to deposit (e.g. GOLD issued amount + XRP drops); their ratio sets the opening price", "TradingFee": "integer 0-1000 = 0%-1% in units of 1/100,000 (e.g. 500 = 0.5%)", "special_fee": "AMMCreate charges a one-time owner-reserve-scaled creation fee (historically ~0.2 XRP class), NOT just the base tx fee — budget for it", "uniqueness": "only ONE AMM may exist per asset pair ledger-wide", "result": "creates AMM object + special AccountRoot (lsfAMMNode), issues LPTokens to creator"}
- **Gotchas:** Seeding at a wrong ratio = wrong opening price that arbitrageurs will instantly drain value from; compute the seed amounts against your intended GOLD price. The creation fee is materially larger than a normal transaction and is non-refundable. You cannot create a second pool for the same pair to 'fix' a mistake — you'd have to AMMWithdraw fully, AMMDelete, and re-create. Both assets must already be held/trustlined by the creating account before AMMCreate.

### AMMDeposit (single & double asset) · `transaction` · ● live ✓
Adds liquidity to an AMM and mints LPTokens to the depositor. Flag-driven modes let you target an exact asset amount, an exact LPToken amount, or a price-capped deposit.

### AMMDeposit single-asset (bootstrap/deepen treasury liquidity) · `transaction` · ● live ✓
How the studio treasury adds liquidity to the GOLD pool AFTER creation without having to pre-balance both sides. Single-asset modes let you inject GOLD-only or XRP-only and receive LPTokens; the protocol charges the pool trading fee on the implicit rebalance (priced as if you traded half the deposit then did a two-asset deposit). Use this to top up depth as the game grows, or to recycle GOLD sinks back into liquidity. For re-seeding a fully-drained pool, tfTwoAssetIfEmpty deposits exact amounts fee-free and resets the price ratio.
- **Chain:** xrpl-mainnet · **XLS:** XLS-30 · **Amendment:** AMM · **Enabled:** 2024-03-22 · **Maturity:** stable · **Use:** recommended
- **Key fields / API:** {"tfSingleAsset (0x00080000)": "deposit exactly the specified amount of ONE asset, get LPTokens (subject to fee)", "tfOneAssetLPToken (0x00200000)": "deposit one asset to receive exactly a target LPToken amount (after fee)", "tfLimitLPToken (0x00400000)": "single-asset deposit with a max effective-price cap = slippage protection", "tfTwoAsset (0x00100000)": "double-asset, fee-free, proportional — use when you can pre-balance", "tfTwoAssetIfEmpty (0x00800000)": "deposit exact amounts into an EMPTY pool, fee-free, sets initial ratio", "tfLPToken (0x00010000)": "double-asset deposit calculated to yield a target LPToken amount"}
- **Gotchas:** Single-asset (tfSingleAsset/tfOneAssetLPToken) is NOT fee-free — a large single-sided GOLD injection eats the trading fee twice over via the implicit swap, so big one-sided deposits self-inflict slippage; prefer tfTwoAsset when you hold both assets, or split a large injection. Always pair single-asset deposits with tfLimitLPToken to cap the effective price or you can overpay in a thin pool. A normal AMMDeposit FAILS on an empty pool (ratio undefined) — you must use tfTwoAssetIfEmpty to revive a drained pool.

### AMMVote — set/govern the swap fee as studio revenue · `transaction` · ● live ✓
The mechanism that sets the GOLD pool's 0%-1% trading fee, which is the studio's direct on-chain revenue line from player swaps: fees accrue to the pool and therefore to LPToken holders pro-rata, so a treasury that holds most LPTokens earns most of the fees on its own token. Fee changes are governed by weighted voting — each LP's vote is weighted by LPTokens held, the pool recomputes its fee as the LPToken-weighted average of the latest votes, and only the top 8 voters (by LPTokens) are counted. While the treasury dominates the pool it effectively sets the fee unilaterally.
- **Chain:** xrpl-mainnet · **XLS:** XLS-30 · **Amendment:** AMM · **Enabled:** 2024-03-22 · **Maturity:** stable · **Use:** recommended
- **Key fields / API:** {"TradingFee": "proposed fee 0-1000 (0%-1% in 1/100,000 units)", "weighting": "vote weight = voter's LPToken balance; result = weighted average of latest votes", "top_8": "at most 8 LPs' votes are counted — top 8 by LPTokens held", "revenue_model": "fees accrue to the pool -> raise LPToken redemption value -> treasury earns its share"}
- **Gotchas:** Fee is capped at 1% — it is a liquidity-incentive knob, not a primary monetization channel; over-fee and players route around your pool via the CLOB. If outside LPs accumulate enough LPTokens they can outvote the treasury (top-8 weighted), so monitor the cap table. Setting fee to 0 removes your revenue but maximizes routing through your pool — a real tradeoff for launch-phase liquidity bootstrapping.
- **Build-focus tracks:** [game-economies](track-game-economies.md)
- **Verify:** cross-family(deepseek-v3.1:671b)=confirmed | net-check: live | currency=current | AMMVote transaction allows LP token holders to govern trading fees on mainnet.
- **Sources:** [Automated Market Makers — AMMVote — XRPL.org](https://xrpl.org/docs/concepts/tokens/decentralized-exchange/automated-market-makers) — Each AMM lets liquidity providers vote on its fee in proportion to LP tokens held via AMMVote; the fee is recalculated as a weighted average and at most the top 8 voters by LP tokens are counted. ; [AMMVote — XRPL.org](https://xrpl.org/docs/references/protocol/transactions/types/ammvote) — AMMVote sets a proposed trading fee (0 to 1000, i.e. 0%-1%) weighted by the voter's LP token holdings.

### Auto-Bridging (XRP as bridge currency) · `concept` · ● live ✓
When two non-XRP currencies have thin direct liquidity, offer crossing automatically routes a synthetic trade through XRP (buy XRP with currency A, sell XRP for currency B) if that yields a better composite rate. It happens automatically on OfferCreate offer crossing, deepening effective liquidity across the long tail of token pairs without any extra action by the trader.
- **Chain:** xrpl-mainnet · **Enabled:** 2012 (genesis) · **Maturity:** stable · **Use:** recommended
- **Key fields / API:** No extra fields - applied automatically during offer crossing; relies on liquidity in both currency/XRP order books
- **Gotchas:** Auto-bridging applies to offer crossing (OfferCreate), NOT to standard Payment transactions by default - cross-currency payments achieve similar effects through pathfinding instead. Benefit depends on XRP-pair depth; if either XRP book is shallow the synthetic route may not win.

### Auto-bridging for cross-currency player trades · `concept` · ● live ✓

### Price Oracle (XLS-47) for game-token price discovery · `standard` · ● live
Native on-ledger price oracles (introduced in rippled 2.2.0): OracleSet creates/updates a PriceOracle ledger entry holding signed price-pair data, OracleDelete removes it, and the get_aggregate_price API returns an aggregated (mean / median / trimmed-mean) price across multiple oracle instances with a standard deviation. For a thin game token this is the safe way to do price discovery: rather than wiring shop prices, reward payouts, or sink costs to the instantaneous AMM spot (trivially manipulable in a shallow pool), publish/consume a smoothed, multi-source, age-checked reference price. ### Permissioned DEX (XLS-81) for regulated game economies · `amendment` · ● live
Credential-gated on-ledger trading, built on XLS-80 Permissioned Domains (enabled 2026-02-04). Offers carry a DomainID tying them to a regulatory domain, and validity depends on the trader holding the right credentials (KYC, region, age). Three offer types: Open (the normal DEX), Permissioned (only credentialed accounts in the domain can place/take), and Hybrid (checks the private permissioned pool first, falls back to the open DEX). For most fun-money game tokens this is overkill — but if the studio runs a real-money, age-gated, or jurisdiction-restricted economy (regulated cash-out, restricted regions, age verification), this is the native way to enforce who may trade GOLD without running an off-ledger gatekeeper.
- **Chain:** xrpl-mainnet · **XLS:** XLS-81 · **Amendment:** PermissionedDEX · **Enabled:** 2026-02-18 · **Maturity:** emerging · **Use:** situational
- **Key fields / API:** {"DomainID": "field on a permissioned offer linking it to a regulatory domain (XLS-80)", "offer_types": "Open / Permissioned / Hybrid", "Hybrid": "matches inside the permissioned domain first, then falls back to the open DEX", "gating": "trade validity requires the account hold the domain's required credentials (KYC/region/age)", "depends_on": "XLS-80 Permissioned Domains (enabled 2026-02-04)"}
- **Gotchas:** Only needed for REGULATED / real-money economies — adding it to a casual game token just fragments liquidity and adds credential plumbing for no benefit. Permissioned offers only match other in-domain participants (except Hybrid fallback), so a small credentialed pool can be very thin. You must run/contract a credential issuer (Permissioned Domains) — the studio becomes responsible for KYC/credential issuance and revocation. Emerging feature (enabled Feb 2026): tooling and SDK support are newer than the core AMM/CLOB; verify library coverage before committing.

### AMMBid — auction slot for discounted-fee market making · `transaction` · ● live ✓
Pay LPTokens to claim a 24-hour 'auction slot' that gives the holder a discounted (down to zero) trading fee on the pool. For a game economy this is the studio's (or a contracted bot's) lever to run arbitrage and price-keeping against the GOLD pool cheaply — keep the pool's spot near the oracle reference without paying the full swap fee each round-trip. The minimum bid for an empty/expired slot is (total LPTokens outstanding x trading fee) / 25; outbidding an active holder costs at least the min plus up to 105% of the current holder's price, and refunds the displaced holder pro-rata by time remaining.
- **Chain:** xrpl-mainnet · **XLS:** XLS-30 · **Amendment:** AMM · **Enabled:** 2024-03-22 · **Maturity:** stable · **Use:** useful
- **Key fields / API:** {"BidMin / BidMax": "bid range in LPTokens", "AuthAccounts": "up to 4 extra accounts that also get the discount (e.g. studio MM bots)", "duration": "slot lasts 24 hours, discounted fee for the holder", "min_bid_empty": "outstanding_LPTokens x trading_fee / 25", "outbid_refund": "displaced holder refunded pro-rata to time elapsed in the 24h window"}
- **Gotchas:** The bid is burned LPTokens (paid back to the pool, benefiting all LPs incl. the studio) — net cost to a dominant treasury is small, but for a minority holder it is real spend. Only worthwhile if there is enough swap volume that the fee discount + arbitrage edge exceeds the bid. The slot discount applies only to the holder + its AuthAccounts, so name your MM bot accounts. Peripheral for a pure casual game economy; matters once volume and arbitrage are real.
