### Vibrato extent and intonation in professional Western lyric singing · `load-bearing`

### Vibrato rate and extent in soprano voice: a survey on one century of singing · `load-bearing`
**More than one hundred recordings of the same tone sung by 75 soprano artists spanning the twentieth century: mean vibrato rate fell by -1.8 ± 0.3 Hz per century while extent rose by 56.4 ± 0.3 cents per century; rate and extent are significantly negatively correlated (r = -0.62); rate increase near the end of the tone was reproduced, along with a mean rise in tone pitch, and note duration correlates weakly positively with extent.
