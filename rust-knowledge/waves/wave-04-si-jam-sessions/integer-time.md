# Integer musical time: ticks, tempo maps, samples — generator packet

Written by `kimi-k2.6:cloud` through `scripts/openrouter_lane.py`; unverified until the wave's verification record says otherwise.

Ticks map to microseconds by the exact rational tick×tempo÷PPQ, computed in u128 and floored once at the end of each tempo segment; carrying the remainder across segments keeps the cumulative time exact. Samples follow by multiplying that result by sample_rate÷1_000_000, or directly tick×tempo×sample_rate÷(PPQ×1_000_000) in u128, again floored after the full rational. PPQ 480, 960 and 3840 are all divisible by 3 and 5, giving exact integer tick deltas for triplets and quintuplets, but none are divisible by 7 so septuplets are inexact. A long take uses u64 for cumulative ticks and u128 for intermediates; at the slowest SMF tempo (0xFFFFFF ≈ 16.78 s/quarter) and PPQ 3840, u64 spans millions of hours before overflow, while u128 intermediates cover any realistic take. Bars and beats under time-signature changes use the SMF FF 58 numerator and power-of-two denominator: ticks_per_beat = PPQ × 4 ÷ 2^denom, ticks_per_bar = ticks_per_beat × num, with integer division exact because all terms are integers. The law refuses overflow via checked_mul and checked_add, returning a refusal rather than wrapping; the release profile should set overflow-checks = true or rely exclusively on checked ops. Wrapping arithmetic is reserved for hash state, never for musical position. u128 compiles and runs on wasm32-unknown-unknown through compiler-builtins, verified by the oracle. No f64 appears in the time path because floating-point seconds round large u64 values and break determinism; integer microseconds and samples are exact.
