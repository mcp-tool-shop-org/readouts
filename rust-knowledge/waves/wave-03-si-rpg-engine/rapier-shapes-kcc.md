# rapier-shapes-kcc: Rapier shapes, meshes and the character controller

**Q1. Which shapes, and how to prepare meshes?** Use TriMesh and HeightField (with FIX_INTERNAL_EDGES) for fixed ground only. Moving bodies get primitives or offline convex_mesh / compound hulls. Meshes are canonicalised, welded and validated offline, because parry 0.30.2 drops topology errors, accepts NaN, panics on bad indices, and welds ±0 by hash layout.

**Q2. How does the controller work at 0.35.3?** move_shape moves nothing. It makes up to 20 casts that stop one offset short, probes a stair on wall hits and slides otherwise, and snaps only from grounded. The caller applies the translation, pushes dynamic bodies with solve_character_collision_impulses, and plans every walker against the last step's poses.

1. **with_flags returns Ok with no topology on a bad mesh, drops a NaN vertex from the AABB, and panics on index 7 of 3.** Dimforge 2026 (parry3d-f64 0.30.2 source, trimesh.rs, https://docs.rs/crate/parry3d-f64/0.30.2/source/src/shape/trimesh.rs). Implication: the loader refuses non-finite values and bad indices itself and takes topology from set_flags. Under panic = "abort", that panic is a trap.
2. **MERGE_DUPLICATE_VERTICES welds a -0.0 copy at 16 vertices but not at 64 (measured): the key hashes raw bytes and compares with ==.** Dimforge 2026 (hashable_partial_eq.rs, https://docs.rs/crate/parry3d-f64/0.30.2/source/src/utils/hashable_partial_eq.rs). Implication: run canon() and weld offline. FIX_INTERNAL_EDGES implies the merge.
3. **convex_hull gives Some(mass 0) for coplanar points, against its error docs, and VHACD split one mesh into 2 parts at resolution 64 and 13 at 16.** Dimforge 2026 (initial_mesh.rs, https://docs.rs/crate/parry3d-f64/0.30.2/source/src/transformation/convex_hull3/initial_mesh.rs). Implication: store hull buffers, load them with convex_mesh, and refuse mass 0.
4. **A trimesh has no interior. An open one has mass 0 and CCD thickness 0.** Dimforge 2026 (Rapier user guide: Colliders, https://rapier.rs/docs/user_guides/rust/colliders). Implication: TriMesh goes on fixed bodies only.
5. **HeightField data is column-major and scale is the whole extent (Rapier's doc says cell size). Each cell is two triangles.** Dimforge 2026 (heightfield3.rs, https://docs.rs/crate/parry3d-f64/0.30.2/source/src/shape/heightfield3.rs). Implication: build_world is right. world.js heightfieldSupport samples bilinearly and reads 0.25 where the solver reads 0.
6. **Internal edges stop a sliding box dead. FIX_INTERNAL_EDGES fixes trimeshes, and a 9×9 heightfield everywhere except row 0 moving +x.** Dimforge 2026 (TriMeshFlags, https://docs.rs/parry3d-f64/0.30.2/parry3d_f64/shape/struct.TriMeshFlags.html). Implication: T4's seam test adopts heightfield_with_flags and crosses row 0 moving +x. A 2-row field is all row 0. The cause is unknown.
7. **Relative defaults scale with the shape, and a walker that does not exclude its own body never moves. is_sliding_down_slope is true on flat ground under gravity, and autostep fires in the air, which the 0.36 guide says it will not.** Dimforge 2026 (character_controller.rs, https://docs.rs/crate/rapier3d-f64/0.35.3/source/src/control/character_controller.rs). Implication: controller()'s Absolute lengths are right. Keep is_sliding_down_slope out of the law.
8. **Only solve_character_collision_impulses moves a dynamic body (crate 0.600 → 2.188). Walkers planned together stay apart at 1–2 u/s and overlap at 8 u/s, whatever the planning order.** Dimforge 2026 (KinematicCharacterController, https://docs.rs/rapier3d-f64/0.35.3/rapier3d_f64/control/struct.KinematicCharacterController.html). Implication: integrate() is right. T4's walker test states its speed and asserts separation, not symmetry.
9. **Line of sight is cast_ray(Ray::new(eye, target − eye), 1.0, true, only_fixed()). Queries see nothing before the first step, and intersect_ray is unsorted.** Dimforge 2026 (QueryPipeline, https://docs.rs/rapier3d-f64/0.35.3/rapier3d_f64/pipeline/struct.QueryPipeline.html). Implication: once meshes arrive, E5 sight needs a law export.
10. **glTF fixes meters, +Y up, float32, unit quaternions and in-range non-restart indices, and ignores a skinned node's transform.** Khronos Group 2026 (glTF 2.0 Specification, https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KhronosGroup/glTF/main/specification/2.0/Specification.adoc). Implication: convert offline with gltf 1.4.1, bake f64 buffers, and refuse the rest.

**Where the evidence is thin.** The row-0 heightfield cause is unexplained. ±0 welding was not measured on wasm32. The Rapier guide pages are labelled 0.36, not 0.35.3. gltf was checked on docs.rs, not compiled. Limit comparisons belong to binary-and-limits.
