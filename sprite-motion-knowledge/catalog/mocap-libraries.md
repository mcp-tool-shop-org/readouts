### Cascadeur (Nekki) — AI-assisted keyframe animation tool with commercial output · `recommended` · ▸ reproduced

Standalone animation software using physics-based auto-posing and AI interpolation to help artists create and polish keyframed animations — it is a production tool, not a pre-built clip library. The studio authors motion FROM SCRATCH or imports reference/BVH and refines it inside Cascadeur. All skeleton types are supported (humanoid, quadruped, exotic) because the artist builds the rig. Pro tier ($396/year) allows unlimited commercial use and perpetual license conversion after 12 months of annual subscription. Free tier: CASC export only, no commercial use. This is the tool that makes exotic-species motion viable: because the artist authors the rig, there is no 'humanoid skeleton mis-fit' problem. Budget Pro tier ($396/year); the perpetual license conversion after 12 months means one annual payment survives subscription lapse for the last released version.
